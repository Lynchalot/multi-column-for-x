# Release notes for addons.mozilla.org ("Version notes" when a version is uploaded)

Short, for the box on the upload page; the full history is CHANGELOG.md.

**First public upload (written at 0.48.7; use the version number you upload):**
- Your X feed in columns on Home, Search, Lists, Bookmarks and profiles. New posts wait behind a "N new" button; nothing jumps.
- A welcome page on install: allow x.com, how to start (Just columns, Calm, Media wall, Reels), the keys (Vim, Simple, none), the colours.
- Posts open in a panel with the picture, the comments, like, bookmark, reply (pictures, GIF, emoji) and translate. Esc puts you back where you were.
- Reels: one post at a time, scrolled up and down. Works on a profile too.
- Vim keys (j k, gg G, w a s d or the arrows over the posts, f like, q copy link, r Reels, ? for the list) or the Simple set; every key can be changed.
- Followers and Following as a grid, a gallery for Media tabs, search on Likes, NSFW only in the Show menu (an option).
- Fourteen colour themes, or X's own colours. Japanese, Korean and Chinese posts use a sans-serif face.
- One-click download of original-size photos and the best video, into a folder and with a file name you choose.
- Click X's logo to fold its menu to icons; Alt+[ and Alt+] too.
- When X changes something the extension depends on, that one feature switches off with a message, or the columns hand back X's own page with a Report button. Settings, Troubleshooting has a table of what is working.
- No data collected, no network requests of its own, no remote code.
