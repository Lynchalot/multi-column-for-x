# Release notes for addons.mozilla.org ("Version notes" when a version is uploaded)

Short, for the box on the upload page; the full history is CHANGELOG.md.

**First public upload (written at 0.48.7; use the version number you upload):**
- Your X feed in columns on Home, Search, Lists, Bookmarks and profiles. New posts wait behind a button; nothing jumps.
- A welcome page that sets it up in a minute: how to start, which keys, which colours.
- Posts open beside their comments. Like, bookmark, reply (with pictures, GIFs and emoji) and translate, then close it and you're back where you were.
- Reels: one post at a time, full screen.
- Keyboard keys, or none, and you can change any of them.
- A gallery for photos, followers as a grid, search on Likes.
- Fourteen colour themes, or X's own colours.
- One-click download of full-size photos and the best video.
- No data collected, no tracking.
