# Trying a build on real X

Nobody working on this can log in to X, so this is the list of what only your own x.com can tell us, in the order that finds the most. Everything is
"does it look right", except 2 and 3, which send me something.

1. **Install and wait ten seconds on the home timeline** with the columns on. Do they draw? Is the Columns pill at the bottom of the menu?
2. **Settings, Troubleshooting, "What is working on x.com".** Screenshot the table. Every "On X's page" row should say *found*. A *not found* is a button or link X has changed.
3. **A post's `...` menu, "Save sample for the developer".** A file goes to your downloads. Open it in an editor (it should hold no words of anyone's), and send it. This is the one thing that lets the tests be made from the real X.
4. **The gear in the columns' bar.** It should open the settings as a panel over the page. If it opens a tab after three seconds instead, X is refusing the frame: send Copy diagnostics. (Tried in a real Firefox against the stand-in, also under a policy that allows frames only from the page's own origin; X's own policy is what is left.)
5. **Fold the menu (press the logo).** The icons should stay in view and the columns move left. Rest the pointer on a few posts for a few seconds each (that starts comment fetches): the menu should not flash names or vanish.
6. **Vimium keys** (j, k, d, u, gg, G) right after loading, then after clicking the left menu, then after clicking the top bar. The columns should scroll each time. (Found broken in Firefox by the real-Firefox test and fixed in 0.32.2: Firefox sends no scroll event for X's page.)
7. **A video: the full-screen button.** It should stay full screen. Esc to leave.
8. **Navigation & sidebar, add Bookmarks, Likes and Lists to the menu.** Do they show their names, in line with the rest?
9. **A profile page:** open a post from it, close it, click around. The header card should stay.
10. **Translate** on a post in another language: time to the result, then "Show original". (`translateTimes` in Copy diagnostics has the numbers.)
11. **Like, Bookmark, Repost** on a post: do they work, and does the table in 2 say *Working* for them afterwards?
12. **The master switch** (Enabled, top of the toolbar panel): off should reload the tab to plain X, with "off" on the toolbar button; on should bring the columns back.
13. **Keys in a post's panel:** open a post. ← → through its pictures and then the posts, Shift+← → the posts only, A like, S bookmark, W repost, E download, Q copy link, C comment (then Esc leaves the box). Do any of them do something else (Vimium, X, Zen)? Which?
14. **Enter from the feed** (nothing focused) opens the first post in view; then the arrows. **The keyboard button** at the corner of a post's panel (the legend), the toast the first time a panel opens, and Settings, Keyboard: change a key and use it. If the toolbar button ever shows a red "!", the extension has no access to x.com.
15. **Going through posts (0.36.0):** pick the **Reels** preset in Settings, open a post with Enter and hold → . Does the next post come up at once (its picture ready)? Does a video play with sound and the next post follow when it ends? Does it keep going past the end of what was drawn, and are posts you have read skipped? Then Posts, *Videos in a post's panel* and *When a video in the panel ends* (Play it again) on their own. Does closing the panel leave the columns at the post you got to?
16. **The comment box's tools (0.37.0):** in a post's panel, press the picture button and attach one or two pictures, add words, and send. Do they arrive on the post as a reply with the pictures? (The upload can take a few seconds; the box says so.) Then the same with no words, a GIF file (it goes alone) and a short video. The emoji button: pick one, then Esc. The GIF button should open X's reply box in a new tab with your words in it. If a picture does not go, send Copy diagnostics, and the outer HTML of X's reply box's file input.
17. **Colour themes (0.40.0):** *Look > Colour theme*, try Catppuccin Mocha and Latte and Gruvbox Dark and Light. Do the columns, the post panel, the viewer and the settings take the colours? Is the left menu's text readable on the page (icons too, and the unread badge)? The right-hand sidebar keeps X's own colours: does it look acceptable beside the theme, in a light one especially? Does X's own page come back when it is set back to *X (as it is)*?
18. **Reels For you / Following (0.40.0):** in Reels on Home with the For you tab not hidden, press the other one at the top of the post: does the first post of that feed come up, without the columns showing in between? Does it stay on For you (and not jump back to Following)?
19. **0.41.0 on a wide screen:** Reels with the Reels preset: does the menu fold and the right panel go while it is up, and come back on Esc? *Look > Text size > Fit to screen* on a very wide window: are the post and its words a good size? Double-click a picture and a video in Reels (the video must not go full screen): does the heart appear and the post get liked? Do the buttons fade after two seconds and return with the mouse? In an ordinary panel, scroll a long comment list: does the slim row with the buttons come at the top, and does Like there work? Does the Colour theme card list look right?
20. **0.41.1:** in Reels on a very wide window, is a normal picture shown whole (with the blurred sides)? A very tall one (a long screenshot): does the wheel scroll it and then go on? Press M with a video in a panel and in the browser's full screen: does it mute and unmute, and does Vimium take the key (change it in Settings, Keyboard if so)?
21. **Reels on a profile (0.42.0):** open someone's profile, press Reels in the bar. Does it start at the first post in view, with the chip ("1 of N") and the Pictures and video / All posts switch? Does the switch change the list and keep your place? Does Esc bring back the grid where you were? Does a post's "..." menu have *Open in Reels from here*?
22. **Vim keys (0.43.0):** Settings, Keyboard, Keys: Vim. On the columns: j and k scroll, gg and G go to the ends, Ctrl+D and Ctrl+U half pages, w a s d move a blue ring over the cards, o opens the ringed one. Is it Zen/Firefox that takes Ctrl+D (bookmark) or Ctrl+U (view source) before the page does? Does X's own j/k selection stay quiet? Do f, b, t, y, c, r, m, ?, and g h / g p go where they should, on the real menu links? Do two keys in a row (gg) show at the bottom left? With Vimium on (and no exclusion), who wins?
23. **Likes search (0.44.0):** open your Likes (your profile's Likes tab). Is there a row under the bar with a search box, a chip for each account, Pictures / Video / Links? Does typing narrow the posts that have loaded and load nothing else? Does *Read older likes* go on (and *Stop* stop it)? On Bookmarks: the chips but no box, and Reels in the bar? On a Search page: Reels in the bar? Is X's History page (Bookmarks and Likes in one) the same route as Bookmarks here, and does the row know which of the two it is on?
24. **Allowing x.com (0.45.0, Firefox / Zen):** with the permission taken away (about:addons, Permissions), does the toolbar button show the "!" and, pressed, open Firefox's prompt with nothing over it? After Allow, do the x.com tabs reload and the popup come back? On a fresh install, is the welcome page's first step the Allow button, and does it turn to a tick?
25. **Gallery and the ring (0.46.0):** on someone's Media tab, switch to Gallery: rows of pictures edge to edge? A press opens the post, pointing offers Reels from there, more load when you reach the end? With the Vim keys: scroll with j, then press s: does the ring start under the mouse (or the middle card) and stay where you are looking, with no jump back?
26. **Followers and Following (0.47.0):** open your Followers (Firefox / Zen). Do the people come as cards in columns with their bios? If not, "Copy sample" from a post's `...` menu on that page is what I need. Is "You don't follow back" right (the cards without a "Following" mark)? Does the search find a bio? On Following, "Doesn't follow you back"? Does a press open the profile?
27. **Profile card (0.48.0):** on someone's profile (scroll the posts a long way first), press **...** on the card: does X's menu come up over the columns, under the button? Press **Following** (0.48.1): does the toast ask "Unfollow @…?", and after you press it does the card say Follow and the toast "Unfollowed @…"? If not, the toast says why (Copy diagnostics has the `header-press`, `unfollow-btn`, `unfollow-saw` and `unfollow` lines in its trace, and `headerButtons`). Also on a profile that has a Subscribe button: the icon next to it (0.48.3) unfollows in one go (toast, press, "Unfollowed @…", and no Subscribe sheet opening), Subscribe opens X's own Subscribe sheet, and ... opens X's menu (if it does not, the toast says so: Copy diagnostics has `header-press` and `header-saw`, and `headerButtons`). Is **Joined** underlined as you point at it? Open the profile's **Reposts** tab: are there columns? (If empty, Copy diagnostics: the ignored requests name what X asks for.)
28. **0.48.0, Zen first:** scroll a long feed with the wheel and the trackpad: lighter than 0.47.0? Is the bar down the columns' right edge gone at rest, showing while you scroll and fading about a second after? With the Vim keys, do the arrow keys move the ring over the posts on the feed? Rest the pointer on a post and press f, b, e: is it that post, with the ring on it? (If it still drags, a Firefox Profiler recording of a few seconds of scrolling is what shows why.) Japanese posts in a gothic (sans) face, not a serif? On a coloured theme, no black box behind the search pill? *Look > Colour theme*: the eight new ones, on the columns, a post's panel and the settings (Dark Academia and Rosé Pine Dawn most of all).
29. **0.48.5:** on a long For you session (scroll a few hundred posts), change the Show menu (Posts only, Media, Everything) and a tab and back: are the posts filled in, not blank boxes? A muted video (the ones that autoplay in the columns) goes round again; press its sound and it plays once. Copy link is q.
30. Anything odd: a post's `...` menu, "Copy diagnostics", and a short recording.

What to send back: the table (2), the sample file (3), Copy diagnostics from anything that failed (it carries a `features` block), and a recording of anything that looked wrong.
